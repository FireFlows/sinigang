The voicebank also comes with some extra phonemes I recorded under a folder called おまけ. Some phonemes can be used for rudimentary English pronunciation.  
* あ・: /ʔ/  
* a 5: /ɫ/  
* a T: /θ/   
* a D: /ð/  
* br吸: inhale  
* br吐: exhale 
* a R: rest
