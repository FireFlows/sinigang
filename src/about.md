## About the Website
I made this website on August 18, 2020 for reasons that are beyond me. I left it alone for six years, and then picked it back up in February of 2026 because I was bored and wanted a space away from social media. I abandoned it for a few more months and came back again in July 2026, so I could host my UTAU here.

While I didn't use generative AI to create this website (code, writing, art done by me or sourced from humans), genAI is forced on me daily by virtue of being on the internet. Despite trying my best to avoid it. We're all going to hell.

### Why don't you have a site profile?

[From Mevdik](https://mevdik.nekoweb.org/guides/neocities-vs-nekoweb/):

> In my extremely subjective opinion which you should treat as such, this whole profile thing is a bad idea. It's presence drags social media attitudes to a website of websites that is supposed to be an escape from the social media hellscape of today. At least that is how a lot of people like to present this platform.

I'm inclined to agree with her. In the future, I intend to create an RSS feed for this website for updates instead.
