Unfortunately I've found that most "deciphering language games" are basically just a simpler form of English (barring a certain language in Chants of Senaar\* + The Expression Amrilato and Distant Memorajo are actually both about Esperanto, a constructed language\*\*). It makes me a little sad, but at the same time it's a lot of effort to make a language, and it's a lot of effort to learn one, too.

\*the Bards' language, which just so happened to be my favorite language, because of how different it is from English. I also love the inspiration from the Devanagari script

\*\*but Esperanto was created so "anyone" could learn it easily. (It's mainly based off of Indo-European/Romance languages ... and it's quite similar to English ...)
