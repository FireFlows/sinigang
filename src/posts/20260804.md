On social media, you'll encounter people who are much more accomplished than you at a much younger age. And I know I shouldn't be comparing myself to them. Comparison is the thief of joy, they say. But I can't help it. 

For example, people who are better at drawing than me. It makes me think, like, what the hell have I been doing? I've been drawing since I was around nine years old, and it's not bad per se. I wouldn't even say it's not "not good" but I could be way better.

I'm still very young. Technically, I'm not even out of my teens yet (some would say the cutoff for teenager ends at 17 but 19 still ends in "teen") but I haven't done as much as I would like. 

I guess there will always be people who are more accomplished than I am. Smarter, more talented, etc. The only thing I'm the best at is being myself. Isn't that nice?
