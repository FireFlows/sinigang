Hi. This was sort of my first year participating in Art Fight. 

I did participate last year, but I only made like two attacks, and I didn't have any OCs. I ended up deleting that account, but I made a new one, since I finally have an OC now. (Naturally, I'm referring to my UTAU.)

And I am so glad I did; it was so much fun! Honestly, I like giving art more than receiving it...!

## Stats

In total, I made 20 attacks. I had a 64.52% battle ratio.

<img src="/assets/img/af26stats.png" alt="My stats for Art Fight 2026. I was on Team Mystery, I got 567 points, I did 12 attacks, 8 friendly fire attacks, and I got 11 defenses." style="max-width:100%;">

I wanted to go for "(at least) one attack each day," but I unfortunately was not able to due to losing my Apple Pencil in the middle of the event. I bought a new one a week later, but in the week I didn't have my Apple Pencil, I ended up getting locked in to fixing up my website and developing my UTAU. So, I didn't draw many attacks after that.

I did draw multiple attacks a day for a couple of days, though, and that was exciting. My hand cramped, but I was mostly able to power through it --- that's what I was locked in to. I drew characters I found interesting, and searched through the UTAU tag often. Since my character is also an UTAU, I wanted to draw other UTAU. (I also just like drawing UTAU fanart.)

Since I love drawing and gifting art to people, I would've been fine if I received no attacks. But I surprisingly received eleven attacks!!! (or defenses, I suppose.) For only having one character, I'd say eleven is pretty good. I'm not particularly popular, nor did I do much advertising... but I'm glad people found my character interesting enough to draw. I want to cherish the drawings people made for me forever!

## Community

Everyone on Art Fight is incredibly nice! Every time someone replied to my art expressing their gratitude, I would smile. My heart would grow a couple of sizes larger, and I'd think, wow, maybe the world doesn't suck after all. Unfortunately the world is not Art Fight sized so it's still shitty, but at least it's not entirely shit.

Writing nice comments to people who drew art for me also made me happy. Even though my comments were often subpar. I'm a writer, but I'm not good at expressing thanks without repeating myself; I often did a generic "thank you! This is so cute!" which I hope to improve on for next year. I genuinely loved every single drawing, it's just difficult to name specific things I like sometimes!

While I advertised my Art Fight on Twitter, I ended up deleting the tweet after a few hours since no one really saw it. I advertised my Art Fight on Tumblr the whole month and that had more success, which is a little oxymoronic because I have 40 followers on Tumblr and 400 on Twitter. I guess many of things don't make sense in this world, though. Or maybe it's because I had it up for a whole month while the Twitter post was only a few hours.

The discussion around Art Fight on Twitter can be rude. In fact, off-site Art Fight discussion in general is a bit of a hellhole. I've seen people repost others' artwork just to criticize it. Even though it was clearly done by a beginner artist. Or minor. Or both. (Even if the artist was experienced and/or an adult, it still wouldn't be OK of course.)

Violating someone's character/global permissions is obviously not OK, but the posts I've seen are like... the violation was most certainly unintentional. And by reposting the artwork to Twitter or Reddit, etc., you're inviting others to bully and harass the artist. Over a mistake.

Honestly, even if permissions were intentionally violated, there's no reason to make a post about it: just report the user for violating your permissions. If someone's acting maliciously (i.e. trolling or ragebaiting), they're going to love the attention your social media post brings.

## Team Mystery 

is probably one of the losing teams this year. I mean, the competition is moreso Comedy/Tragedy, Mystery's just the third wheel. The winning team will likely be Comedy, as it's the team the Big H (Andrew Hussie) is on. You know, the creator of the webcomic Problem Sleuth?

But I think it's really great they participated! I feel like people were being too harsh on them for participating. Why can't a famous artist participate in an art trading game? Just because someone's famous doesn't mean they aren't a person.

When Art Fight started and they hadn't made any attacks yet, people were criticizing them for "farming fanart." Seriously!? I'm sure they don't need to look very hard for fanart.

Also! Even if they were farming fanart, I don't think that's a real issue. It's good to give back, but it's an art _gifting_ game, not an art _trading_ game. If you're attacking people with the intention of getting revenges, you're going to lose your mind. And if someone has no attacks but a bunch of defenses, and you don't like that, you can simply not draw their OC.

People love to hate. Maybe Andrew Hussie's done some not-so-good things in their two decades+ of being online but this isn't one of them. This is literally fine. Bye. I love to fucking hate all of you.

## Art Fight 2027 

I've already started thinking about Art Fight 2027. Hopefully, my UTAU's voicebank will be released by then, so that will be fun! I want to record another UTAU in the future, too. And maybe I'll put them in Art Fight too.

I want to do "(at least) one attack per day" for real, next time. I'm so excited thinking about next years' Art Fight even though it's a year away!

Previously, I never really had OCs so I didn't understand Art Fight too much, but now I get it. I understand why people like it so much. It is so much fun.
