## Generative artificial intelligence
<dl>

<dt><a href="https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/chatgpt-and-gemini-ai-have-uniquely-different-writing-styles/">Each AI Chatbot Has Its Own Distinctive Writing Style—Just as Humans Do</a>
<dd>An article about chatbot idiolects. Also see "<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12150v2">Idiosyncrasies in Large Language Models</a>," a paper diving deeper into the topic.

<dt><a href="https://resrc.neocities.org/posts/how-to-tell-when-a-website-is-ai">How to tell when a website is AI</a> 
<dd>A quick guide on how to spot AI-generated websites. Includes a tester that checks for common AI coding tells

<dt><a href="https://archive.org/details/fandom-has-a-hidden-generative-ai-problem-1">Fandom has a hidden Generative AI Problem</a>
<dd>An easy way to tell if writing was generated by Claude. Also see <a href="https://x.com/heatedrivalryai/status/2071719219960426605">original thread on Twitter</a>

<dt><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing">Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing</a>
<dd>Wikipedians lay out some features of "writing" generated with artificial intelligence. Mainly focused on Wikipedia-specific signs but can be used more generally.

</dl>
